## Epic C — Coach sharing (`docs/product/coach-sharing.md`): sharing a trainee's progress with their personal trainer, where the trainer refers several patients and may be the one paying. Design written 2026-08-05. ~~C-1 coach aggregate + patient link~~ (✅ 2026-08-05: migration 32, one active trainer per patient enforced by a partial unique index, append-only revoke-only links so past referrals stay answerable, patient-list filter by coach, "trainees of X" view carrying identity only, Entrenador tab on the expediente, T-36/T-37). Building it corrected the design: a referral link authorises NOTHING and carries no consent id — recording who someone trains with is administrative record-keeping, and folding the consent into it would have meant she could not note a trainer without a consent form while quietly turning a note into a licence to share. ~~C-2 `third_party_transfer` consent wired at last~~ (✅ 2026-08-05: migration 33 `coach_share_grants`, consent_id NOT NULL + UNIQUE so one consent authorises exactly one coach and a blanket consent is unrepresentable, enumerated scope columns, effectiveness NEVER stored but re-derived from grant+link+consent on every read so a withdrawal stops sharing instantly, refusals return an EMPTY scope, per-patient "who could see my data" history in the export and the UI, T-38. Also fixed a Phase-2 misclassification found here: `consentWithdraw` was gated by the licence, so a lapsed subscription would have refused to record a patient's withdrawal — taking permission away is now never gated, granting still is). ~~C-3 the coach pack~~ (✅ 2026-08-05: coach report PDF scoped in the application layer so out-of-scope values never reach the DTO, authorisation block naming the coach + consent + notice version + what the document never contains, provenance repeated on every page, batch generation per coach that lists every skipped trainee and why, T-39). ~~Sweep of C-2/C-3~~ (✅ 2026-08-06, two gaps, both confirmed with a probe first: photos kept flowing to a trainer after the patient withdrew the PHOTO consent — the app refuses to accept a photo without it, so `evaluateCoachShare` now requires both consents and narrows the scope rather than refusing the whole report, refusing outright only when photos were all that was granted; and an archived trainee with a live authorisation appeared in neither the pack's reports nor its skip list while her individual report still built, so the pack now iterates every live link and skips her with `patient_archived`). Remaining: C-4 coach portal (publish-snapshot, not sync — a project, not a slice). The recommendation is C-1..C-3 only: they are where the value is, they add no egress, and they make C-4 cheap if a trainer ever demands it. A coach is deliberately **not** a user of the app and can never write, which is what keeps this out of the multi-tenancy project (Epic S, S-4). Decisions needed in §8 of the design doc; the one that changes the shape of the work is whether the trainer needs live access or a report she sends.
